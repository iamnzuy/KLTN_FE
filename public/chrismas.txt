Dear teachers and classmates,

Christmas is one of the special holidays celebrated all around the world. It is a season of giving, spreading kindness, and cherishing moments of togetherness. Christmas brings joy and warmth, and reminds us to appreciate the simple things in life.

With this spirit, our class, 8C3, decided to organize a small and cozy Christmas party in our classroom. We hope this party can create a friendly and cheerful space where everyone can enjoy food together, share laughter, and feel the Christmas atmosphere.

Now, we would like to introduce the dishes on the Christmas party table (chỉ tay tới cái bàn - over heree)

First, we have roasted chicken. It is a familiar dish at many Christmas parties, with its golden color and delicious smell. It is also the main dish of our party and represents warmth and togetherness.

Next are fried fermented pork rolls and grilled meat skewers. These are also familiar dishes that are easy to eat and suitable for the tastes of students.

We also have Korean-style spring rolls, ham with sliced bread, and some boiled vegetables to make the meal more balanced.

Besides that, rice cakes, with their light flavor, soft texture, and easy-to-eat nature, are well suited to the cheerful atmosphere of the party. Soft drinks and fresh fruits are prepared for everyone to refresh themselves and enjoy dessert, helping to make the party more complete.

Most importantly, the highlight of the party is the Christmas-themed cake. It is not only beautiful but also meaningful, showing our wishes for a happy, warm, and peaceful Christmas for everyone.

Through this small party, class 8C3 would like to send our best wishes to all teachers and classmates.
We wish everyone a peaceful, warm, and joyful Christmas.

Thank you very much for your attention.
